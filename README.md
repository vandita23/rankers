# KisanAI

## AI-Powered Agricultural Assistance Platform for Indian Farmers

KisanAI is an AI-powered agricultural assistance platform designed to provide Indian farmers with accessible, practical, and localized support for everyday farming decisions.

The platform brings multiple agricultural services into a single application, including AI-powered agricultural assistance, crop disease detection, weather intelligence, government scheme discovery, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

---

## Problem Statement

Farmers often need to make important decisions regarding crop health, irrigation, weather conditions, fertilizers, diseases, and government assistance.

However, agricultural information is often:

- Distributed across multiple platforms
- Difficult to understand
- Not localized to the farmer's context
- Available in technical language
- Difficult to access quickly
- Not available through a single unified platform

Crop diseases can also be difficult to identify without expert assistance. Changing weather conditions can affect irrigation, crop health, and farming decisions. At the same time, many farmers may not be aware of government schemes and financial assistance available to them.

KisanAI addresses these challenges by bringing multiple agricultural intelligence capabilities together into one platform.

---

## Our Solution

KisanAI acts as a unified digital agricultural assistant.

Farmers can use the platform to:

- Ask agricultural questions using natural language
- Receive AI-assisted farming guidance
- Upload crop images for disease identification
- Check weather information
- Discover government agricultural schemes
- Retrieve information from an agricultural knowledge base
- Interact in English and Hindi

Instead of requiring farmers to search across multiple applications and websites, KisanAI provides these capabilities through one integrated platform.
