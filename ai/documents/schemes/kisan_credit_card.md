# Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Category: Agricultural credit / Government scheme

Kisan Credit Card is an agricultural credit facility intended to help eligible farmers meet farming-related working-capital and other approved agricultural needs.

Demo knowledge-base eligibility information:
- Farmers
- Tenant farmers and sharecroppers where applicable
- Eligible self-help group members engaged in farming where applicable

Common documents listed in this demo knowledge base:
- Aadhaar or other identity proof
- Land or cultivation documents as applicable
- Passport-size photograph

Application information in this demo knowledge base:
- Visit a participating bank branch
- Complete the KCC application
- The bank verifies the application and applicable documents

Source: Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare
