# PM-KISAN

Category: Government scheme

PM-KISAN is a farmer income-support scheme. The KisanAI demo knowledge base describes it as providing income support to eligible farmer families.

Eligibility information in this demo knowledge base:
- Small and marginal farmer family
- Own cultivable land
- Valid land records

Common documents listed in this demo knowledge base:
- Aadhaar card
- Land ownership papers
- Bank account details

Application information in this demo knowledge base:
- Visit pmkisan.gov.in or a nearby Common Service Centre (CSC)
- Fill the registration form
- Submit documents for verification

Source: pmkisan.gov.in
