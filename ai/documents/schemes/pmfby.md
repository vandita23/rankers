# Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Category: Crop insurance / Government scheme

PMFBY is a crop-insurance scheme intended to provide coverage for eligible notified crops against specified crop losses, including losses associated with natural calamities, pests and diseases, subject to the applicable scheme rules and notifications.

Demo knowledge-base eligibility information:
- Farmers growing notified crops
- Loanee and non-loanee farmers may be covered subject to applicable rules

Common documents listed in this demo knowledge base:
- Aadhaar card
- Land records
- Bank passbook
- Sowing declaration

Application information in this demo knowledge base:
- Contact the relevant bank, CSC or official PMFBY channel
- Complete the applicable insurance application
- Report crop loss promptly through the prescribed channel and within the applicable deadline

Important: Exact eligibility, notified crops, premium, claim process and reporting deadlines can vary by crop, state and season. Verify current rules through official PMFBY or government sources before acting.

Source: pmfby.gov.in
