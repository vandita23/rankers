# Wheat stripe rust — demo disease knowledge

Wheat stripe rust is a fungal disease associated with yellow-orange stripe-like pustules on wheat leaves. Cool and humid conditions can favor disease development.

If symptoms are suspected:
- Inspect additional plants in the field rather than relying on one leaf.
- Record the affected crop variety and field conditions.
- Follow locally approved disease-management recommendations for the crop and region.
- Consult a qualified agricultural expert before applying a fungicide or other treatment.

The AI should not prescribe a chemical, dose or spray schedule unless a verified, current source is available for the farmer's crop, location and conditions.
