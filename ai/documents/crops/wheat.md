# Wheat — demo crop knowledge

Wheat management depends on variety, sowing date, soil, irrigation, local weather and pest or disease pressure.

For disease or treatment decisions, farmers should verify recommendations with a local agricultural extension officer, Krishi Vigyan Kendra or other qualified expert.

Common monitoring points include plant stand, soil moisture, irrigation timing, nutrient symptoms and visible leaf diseases. Avoid making pesticide decisions from a single symptom alone.
